Core Curriculum Topics

Key learning modules covered throughout BIOL 217

Cell Membrane & Transport

Fluid mosaic model, lipid bilayers, membrane proteins, active and passive transport, and ion channels.

Weeks 1–3 Module 1

Bioenergetics & Metabolism

Enzyme kinetics, ATP synthesis, cellular respiration, mitochondrial electron transport, and photosynthesis.

Weeks 4–7 Module 2

Gene Expression & Regulation

Transcription, RNA processing, translation, epigenetic modifications, and operon regulatory networks.

Weeks 8–12 Module 3

Recombinant Biotech & CRISPR

PCR, cloning vectors, DNA sequencing technologies, genomic editing, and real-world biotech applications.

Weeks 13–15 Module 4
